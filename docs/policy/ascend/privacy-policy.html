Ascend

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 22, 2026

🔒 Your streak stays on your phone

Ascend is a self-improvement streak tracker developed by Dream Apps Labs (owned and operated by Shivam, an individual software developer). There is no account, sign-up or login. Your streak, history, pledges, journal entries, reset notes and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this data, and we never see it.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following locally, inside its private app storage:

  • Streak start times, past streaks and the reasons you choose when ending a streak.
  • Journal entries, moods, daily pledges, quests, badges, XP and levels.
  • Your settings, such as reminder time, theme and your personal "why".

This information is never sent to Dream Apps Labs and is never shared with advertisers. If Android backup is enabled on your phone, Android may include the app's data in your own device backup in your Google account, as it does for other apps. You can turn this off in your Android backup settings.

2. Advertising (free version)

The free version of Ascend shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. Ascend Pro removes all ads. To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Device and app information (for example, device model, OS version, and that the request comes from Ascend).
  • Ad interactions and diagnostic data, such as whether an ad loaded or was tapped.

Google uses this information under its own policies, including to personalise ads where permitted: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

None of your streak, journal or reset data is passed to AdMob. Ads in Ascend are restricted to general-audience content.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): Before ads are shown, Ascend displays Google's consent message where the law requires it. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • Ad personalisation: You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • No ads at all: Ascend Pro removes ads, and the ad SDK is not started for Pro users.

3. Purchases

Ascend Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether you have an active purchase so it can unlock Pro features. [Google Privacy Policy]

4. Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, Ascend schedules a local notification on your device. Reminders are deliberately discreet and do not mention what you are tracking. No notification content is sent to any server.

5. What We Do Not Collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location.

6. Data Control and Deletion

  • Use Settings → Erase all data in the app to permanently delete your streak, history and journal from the device.
  • Uninstalling the app also permanently deletes all of its local data.
  • For data processed by Google for advertising, use the choices in section 2 or visit My Ad Center.

7. Age Requirement

Ascend is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly process information from anyone under 18.

8. Not Medical Advice

Ascend is a self-improvement tool and does not provide medical, psychological or therapeutic advice. If a habit is seriously affecting your life, please consider speaking with a licensed professional.
