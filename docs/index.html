Our Privacy Philosophy

The standard principles applied across every tool created by Dream Apps Labs.

🔒

Zero-Cloud Storage

All data stays securely inside local Room SQLite databases on your device. We operate zero cloud servers, customer accounts, or backend telemetry pipelines.

⚡

Local On-Device AI

When our applications leverage machine learning, models run directly on your hardware with ultra-low latency, zero network traffic, and no cloud dependency.

🛡️

Play Store Compliance

We respect Android security models by utilizing non-intrusive APIs (such as Notification Interception) rather than broad, risky device permissions.

💾

Encrypted Backups

Optional backup syncing connects directly to your personal Google Drive with client-side AES-256 encryption. We never hold your decryption keys.

A Studio Dedicated to Honest Software

Our business model is built on providing real utility through optional lifetime passes and transparent tools—never by monetizing personal information.
